
# Mezzo Product Philosophy

## Mission

Mezzo helps people become more aware of how they communicate by providing calm, real-time feedback about their speaking volume.

Rather than correcting behavior through alerts or analytics, Mezzo encourages self-awareness through ambient visual feedback.

---

# Philosophy

Mezzo is designed around a simple belief:

> The best technology quietly supports people without becoming the center of attention.

It should feel less like software and more like an instrument that sits beside the user while they work.

---

# Principles

## Ambient, Not Interruptive

Mezzo communicates through subtle changes rather than notifications, sounds, or warnings.

The user should discover information naturally rather than being interrupted.

---

## Awareness Over Enforcement

Mezzo never tells users they are "wrong."

Instead, it reflects where they are relative to the speaking profile they intentionally selected.

The user remains in control.

---

## Privacy First

Voice never leaves the device.

No conversations are recorded.

No transcripts are generated.

No audio is stored.

Mezzo exists only to provide real-time feedback.

---

## Calm by Default

Silence should look calm.

Normal conversation should look balanced.

Only sustained deviation should attract attention.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Information accumulates.

The interface never jumps abruptly between unrelated states.

Every new visual cue builds upon the previous one.

---

## Peripheral Awareness

Users should rarely need to look directly at Mezzo.

Motion, rhythm, and subtle visual hierarchy should allow users to notice changes from the corner of their eye.
