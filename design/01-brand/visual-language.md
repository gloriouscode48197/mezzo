# Visual Metaphor

The interface represents a field of resonance.

The center represents the speaker.

Contours represent equilibrium.

Motion represents energy.

Color represents sustained deviation.

---

# Shape Language

Default geometry is circular and balanced.

As vocal energy increases, geometry gradually loses equilibrium through subtle organic deformation.

No sudden visual changes.

Everything evolves continuously.

---
## Natural Resonance

During the development of Mezzo's monitoring visualization, an unexpected pattern emerged.

As the interface evolved away from rigid geometric circles toward softer organic contours, the resulting forms began to resemble naturally occurring nested structures—including the folds of the human ear.

This observation reinforced an important realization:

> Mezzo is fundamentally about listening, not measuring.

The visualization is **not intended to depict an ear**, nor should it ever become a recognizable icon.

Instead, it borrows from the characteristics found in natural listening structures:

- nested contours
- subtle asymmetry
- varying spacing
- continuous flowing geometry
- organic equilibrium rather than mathematical perfection

These characteristics help the visualization feel calm, familiar, and alive without becoming literal.

The goal is to evoke the feeling of resonance moving through a living system rather than information being displayed on a meter.

# Empty Space

Whitespace is considered part of the interface.

The visualization should never fill the entire PiP.

Silence is expressed through space.
