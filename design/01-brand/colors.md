
# Mezzo Color System

## Philosophy

Color should reinforce information rather than compete for attention.

Motion communicates first.

Color communicates second.

---

# Palette

## Background

Charcoal Black

Used for application background.

---

## Surface

Warm Dark Gray

Used for cards and elevated surfaces.

---

## Primary Text

Soft White

High contrast without feeling harsh.

---

## Secondary Text

Muted Warm Gray

Used for labels and supporting information.

---

## Accent

Mezzo Orange

Reserved for:

- active monitoring indicator
- outside range
- primary actions

The accent color should feel warm rather than alarming.

---

# Usage Rules

Orange should be rare.

If everything is orange, nothing is important.

Most of the interface should remain neutral.
