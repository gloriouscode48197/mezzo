
# Motion Principles

## Motion Has Meaning

Animation should always communicate state.

Avoid decorative movement.

---

# Breathing

Represents presence.

The system is active.

Not listening.

Simply alive.

---

# Propagation

Represents detected speech.

Propagation should only occur when vocal energy exists.

---

# Morphing

Represents deviation from equilibrium.

Distortion increases gradually.

---

# Rhythm

Motion cadence increases as vocal energy increases.

Never use abrupt transitions.

---

# Continuity

Animations should feel like one continuous physical system.

Not separate effects layered together.

---

# Peripheral Awareness

Motion should attract attention gently.

The user should notice changes without needing to stare at the visualization.
