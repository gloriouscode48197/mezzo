# Discoveries

> *A journal of observations, realizations, and design insights that influenced Mezzo.*

Unlike design decisions, discoveries are moments of learning.

Some become permanent product principles.

Others simply change how future problems are approached.

# Example

## Date

2026-07-11

---

## Context

Refining the PiP monitoring visualization.

---

## Discovery

The visualization began to resemble the nested structure of a human ear.

This reinforced that Mezzo is fundamentally about listening rather than measuring.

---

## Why It Matters

Until this point the visualization had been treated as an animated indicator.

Thinking about listening shifted the goal toward creating a living field that quietly receives and reflects energy.

---

## Design Implications

- Favor nested organic contours.
- Avoid perfectly uniform spacing.
- Let propagation inherit the current contour shape.
- Keep the visualization abstract rather than representational.

---

## Next Questions

Can the same principles influence the rest of the product, including onboarding and calibration?

# Writing Guidelines

Good discoveries usually begin with phrases like:

- "We realized..."
- "It became clear that..."
- "The design felt wrong because..."
- "The breakthrough was..."
- "We stopped asking..."
- "Instead of..."
- "The visualization should..."

A discovery captures a shift in understanding—not simply a design change.

If a discovery continues to prove true over time, it should eventually be promoted into a permanent design principle.
