How Range reads: the circle is your voice and the boundary is the end of your range, so you can always see how much room you have. Inside the boundary your voice stays the circle's own color. Past it, a soft light band grows outside the boundary, in proportion to how far over you are, and shrinks as you ease off. Stone keeps it muted with a white band; Lime and Pink keep the gray but make the band an accent color; Night changes the background too. In every palette the voice circle stays neutral, so the accent means only one thing, and the band stays lighter than everything around it, so it still reads for color-blind viewers.
