Privacy & Data Use

Mezzo is designed to provide volume awareness without collecting or storing sensitive information. We believe trust depends as much on what a system doesn't do as what it does.

Audio handling

  • Mezzo listens only to audio volume levels, not speech content
  • Audio is processed locally in your browser
  • No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted
  • Mezzo does not transcribe speech or use AI to analyze content

Monitoring runs only while Mezzo is open, and you can stop it at any time.

Speaking profiles

Speaking profiles are stored only in your browser.

  • Mezzo saves only the profile name and a derived calibration value, so you can reuse it later without recalibrating
  • Your voice is never recorded or stored. Mezzo does not save audio recordings, microphone input, voice samples, or transcripts.
  • Your profiles never leave your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, synced between devices, or shared with anyone.
  • You don't need an account to use Mezzo or save profiles

Anonymous usage data

Mezzo uses Mixpanel, an analytics service, to count a few anonymous usage events. Besides one that registers the anonymous identifier below, it sends only these:

  • Mezzo opened, and whether this browser has saved speaking profiles
  • A calibration finished
  • Monitoring started
  • Monitoring ended, with how long it lasted and whether the glow appeared

Never audio, volume levels or what you say. Each event also carries the page address, the site you came from on your first visit, your browser, operating system and screen size, as Mixpanel sends by default. Mixpanel receives your IP address with each request, and Mezzo tells it not to use that address to estimate your location. Study codes are removed from the page address before Mixpanel starts.

On your first visit, Mezzo creates a random, anonymous identifier and keeps it in your browser’s local storage. Mezzo sets no cookies. The identifier isn’t tied to your name or email, isn’t shared across devices, and isn’t shared with other websites, except in a study check-in (see Research participants). We use it only to answer questions such as how often Mezzo is used and whether people come back.

Usage data never includes names, email addresses or account information.

Research participants

If you join a Mezzo research study, you’ll get a personal study link. After you stop monitoring, or if the Mezzo Companion closes during a call, Mezzo shows a link to a short survey. Check-ins are collected through Tally, a survey tool. If you sign up for a study, Tally stores the name and email you enter, used only to contact you about the study.

The survey link carries your study code, your anonymous identifier and, for a recent session, the date, how long you monitored, for how many of those minutes Mezzo heard speech, how many times the glow appeared, and which Companion view you used and its window size. Tally receives these when the survey opens, and saves them as a response only if you submit it.

Because it’s the same anonymous identifier, a submitted check-in can be matched with that browser’s usage events. Mezzo only notes whether the volume was loud enough to count as speech, never what was said. Mezzo never sends audio. If you’re not in a study, you won’t see the survey.

Chrome extension

The Mezzo extension checks Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams pages for a “leave call” button to tell when you’re in a call, then offers to open Mezzo. It doesn’t use your microphone, doesn’t read meeting content, and doesn’t store or send any data.

What Mezzo does not do

  • No recording or storage of audio
  • No transcription or keyword detection
  • No cookies, and no cross-site or cross-device tracking
  • No sale of data. The only outside services are Mixpanel, for the anonymous usage events above, and Tally, for study check-ins. Like any website, Mezzo’s host, Vercel, receives your IP address when a page loads. Mezzo’s fonts come from its own site.

Your control

Because Mezzo runs entirely in the browser:

  • Closing the tab stops monitoring
  • Clearing your browser data removes the anonymous identifier
  • Clearing your browser data also removes any saved speaking profiles
  • You are always in control of when Mezzo is active
