Privacy & Data Use

Mezzo is designed to provide volume awareness without collecting or storing sensitive information. We believe trust depends as much on what a system doesn't do as what it does.

Audio handling

  • Mezzo listens only to audio volume levels, not speech content
  • Audio is processed locally in your browser
  • No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted
  • Mezzo does not transcribe speech or use AI to analyze content

Monitoring runs only while Mezzo is open, and you can stop it at any time.

Speaking profiles

Speaking profiles are stored only in your browser.

  • Mezzo saves only the profile name and a derived calibration value, so you can reuse it later without recalibrating
  • Your voice is never recorded or stored. Mezzo does not save audio recordings, microphone input, voice samples, or transcripts.
  • Your profiles never leave your device. They are never uploaded to our servers, synced between devices, or shared with anyone.
  • You don't need an account to use Mezzo or save profiles

Anonymous usage data

Mezzo collects minimal, anonymous usage data to understand how the product is being used and to improve its effectiveness.

On your first visit, Mezzo generates a random, anonymous identifier and stores it locally in your browser. This identifier is not tied to your identity, is not shared across devices, and is not shared with other websites, except in a study check-in you choose to submit (see Research participants).

We use this identifier only to answer high-level questions such as:

  • How often Mezzo is used
  • Whether people return to use it again
  • Which features are used over time

We do not collect names, email addresses, account information, or any personally identifiable data.

Research participants

If you join a Mezzo research study, you’ll get a personal study link. After you stop monitoring, or if the Mezzo Companion closes during a call, Mezzo shows a link to a short survey. Check-ins are collected through Tally, a survey tool. If you submit one, the survey also records your study code, your anonymous identifier, the date, how long you monitored, for how many of those minutes Mezzo heard speech, how many times the glow appeared, and which Companion view you used and its window size. Mezzo only notes whether the volume was loud enough to count as speech, never what was said. Mezzo never sends audio. If you’re not in a study, you won’t see the survey.

What Mezzo does not do

  • No recording or storage of audio
  • No transcription or keyword detection
  • No cross-site or cross-device tracking
  • No sale of data. Data is never shared with third parties, except study check-ins you choose to submit through the survey tool, Tally.

Your control

Because Mezzo runs entirely in the browser:

  • Closing the tab stops monitoring
  • Clearing your browser data removes the anonymous identifier
  • Clearing your browser data also removes any saved speaking profiles
  • You are always in control of when Mezzo is active
